                  Calendar

                  See your meetings on the Plan page, how much focus time is left, and get a reminder before each meeting. You can connect several calendars; their meetings are combined.

                    Works with the classic Outlook desktop app. Steady reads your calendar directly; nothing leaves your computer. If you use the new Outlook, add a calendar link instead.

                    In Outlook on the web, go to Settings, then Calendar, then Shared calendars, and publish your calendar to get an ICS link. In Google Calendar, use "Secret address in iCal format". Keep the link private; anyone with it can see your calendar.
